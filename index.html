01 / PROFILE

About me

I am a Doctoral Researcher in Software Engineering at the School of Computer Science and Technology, Beijing Institute of Technology (BIT), China.

My research focuses on software security, automated software maintenance, and program analysis. I investigate how large language models (LLMs), static analysis, and graph-guided reasoning can support the identification and classification of security-related code changes and the synthesis of executable verification rules.

My current work explores silent security patch identification, weakness-informed verification, and fine-grained Common Weakness Enumeration (CWE) classification. Previously, at Xi’an Jiaotong University, I worked on requirements engineering, user-feedback mining, rationale mining, and applied natural language processing.

I am interested in research collaborations and discussions at the intersection of artificial intelligence, software engineering, and cybersecurity.

02 / CURRENT WORK

Research projects

Selected research on intelligent software analysis and security.

01Published · ASE 2025

AACC

Augmented Agentic Commit Classification

An LLM-based approach for identifying Null Pointer Exception (NPE)-fixing commits. The work investigates context-aware example selection, an augmented knowledge base, agent-based commit prioritization, and iterative feedback-driven refinement.

Read the ASE 2025 paper ↗
02Under review · FSE 2027

WISE-Fix

Weakness-Informed Executable Verification

A framework for identifying silent security patches in C/C++ repositories. It synthesizes executable verifiers offline using LLMs and applies weakness-informed verification to assess security-fix behavior.

Manuscript submitted to the ACM International Conference on the Foundations of Software Engineering (FSE 2027).

03Under review · IEEE TSE

CWEMap

Graph-Guided Reasoning for Fine-Grained CWE Classification

A graph-guided approach to classifying security patches by CWE category, combining knowledge-graph-guided reasoning with VF2++ graph matching to support fine-grained weakness classification.

Manuscript under review at IEEE Transactions on Software Engineering (TSE).

Additional research: Security-patch dataset curation and benchmarking using PatchDB*, SPI-DB*, Defects4J, and Vul4J.
